What the Device Management System is

The Nigerian Communications Commission (the Commission) now keeps a register of every phone allowed on Nigerian networks, called the Device Management System. A phone is identified by its IMEI number — the 15-digit number that appears when you dial *#06#. Most phones have two IMEI numbers, one for each SIM slot. If a phone's IMEI numbers are not on the register, the Commission sends a 30-day warning by text message, after which the phone can be cut off.

A person holding one phone registers it free by calling 315. Every phone sold in Nigeria is the seller's duty to register — the Commission's own words are that the duties “fall on the relevant businesses in the supply chain” — and only a company holding the Commission's Sales and Installation licence can register in bulk. TechTanks registers on behalf of dealers, importers and manufacturers.

How it works — for a dealer

One shop, four hundred phones, six steps

The text messages start.

Customers come back to the shop: “Your device is not registered. You have 30 days.” The Commission's answer is that the seller is responsible for registering phones sold in Nigeria.

List the stock.

Send us IMEI numbers and model numbers — a spreadsheet, photographs of the boxes, or we scan the shelf. Most phones carry two numbers; both are listed.

Each model against the Commission's Type Approval register. Unapproved models are flagged and routed to a Type Approval filing; nothing is charged for them.

Frequently asked questions

I got the text. What do I do?

Call 315. Read out your IMEI number (dial *#06# to see it). The Commission raises a ticket and tells you by text and email when the phone is registered. It is free, and the line is open almost round the clock. You do not need us for one phone.

What is the difference between Type Approval and registration?

Type Approval is the Commission's certificate for a model of phone, done once by the manufacturer or an agent. Registration puts one phone's IMEI numbers on the Device Management System. A phone cannot be registered unless its model is Type Approved.

I sell phones. Can my customers just call 315?

They can, one phone at a time, and many will. But the Commission says the duty to register phones sold in Nigeria is the seller's, and 315 does not register a shop's stock. Dealer Registration does.

Why do you charge per IMEI number and not per phone?

The Commission's fee is per IMEI number. Most phones have two, so a phone with two IMEI numbers is registered twice.

Do you need receipts or National Identification Numbers?

No. For a dealer we need the list of IMEI numbers and model numbers. We never ask an individual for a National Identification Number, a receipt or bank details.

What if a model is not Type Approved?

We tell you, and nothing is charged for those phones. The manufacturer or an importer must apply for Type Approval first — see our Type Approval service.

How do I know this is not a scam?

The Commission has warned against unverified third-party registration sites. We never charge individuals — we send them to 315.
